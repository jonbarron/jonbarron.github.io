Sajad Hamzenejadi

PhD Student · Information Systems · University of Geneva

I'm a PhD student in Information Systems at the University of Geneva (UNIGE). Previously, I was a research intern at Nokia Bell Labs in Paris, France, and I received my MSc in Telecommunications Engineering from Politecnico di Milano.

My research interests lie at the intersection of machine learning and networked systems, including AI for cybersecurity (mobile botnet and malware detection), network traffic forecasting with generative models, and signal processing.
